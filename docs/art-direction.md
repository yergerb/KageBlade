# Art Direction

## North Star

Kageblade should feel like an original neon anime weapon fighter built for OLED screens: deep blacks, saturated attacks, readable silhouettes, and crunchy arcade impact.

## Style Pillars

- **OLED contrast:** dark navy, black, and deep crimson bases with bright cyan, magenta, gold, and white highlights.
- **Weapon readability:** oversized silhouettes and long slash trails that communicate range instantly.
- **Anime arcade energy:** dramatic poses, strong line weight, punchy effects, and exaggerated impact frames.
- **Chunky premium sprites:** hand-pixelated edges mixed with cel-shaded interior detail.
- **Dark stages:** backgrounds stay lower contrast so fighters, weapons, sparks, and UI pop.

## Character Art Rules

- Keep every fighter full-body and side-view readable.
- Use thick dark outlines on the body and weapon.
- Avoid tiny costume detail that disappears in motion.
- Give each fighter one dominant accent color and one secondary accent color.
- Push weapons larger than realistic scale, but keep the contact point clear.
- Align animation frames to a consistent ground baseline.

## Effects Rules

- Slashes should be brighter than the fighter.
- Hits need a white-hot center, colored outer sparks, and a short freeze.
- Parries should feel sharp and premium: cyan/white flash, ring burst, brief time stop.
- Blocks should be cooler and flatter than hits, with blue shield sparks.
- Avoid muddy smoke. Dust should be dark with bright rim flecks.

## Background Rules

- Start with one rooftop night arena.
- Keep the playable floor dark and reflective.
- Put the brightest environment lights behind or above the fighters, not over them.
- Use distant pagodas, torii silhouettes, wet stone, lantern glow, rain streaks, and parallax layers.
- Do not let background details compete with attack silhouettes.
